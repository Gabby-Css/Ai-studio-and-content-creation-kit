# Add your own licensed reference media

The redistributable release omits third-party video and image binaries from `video-library/` and
`arsenal-library/`. The written craft studies and tool guides remain so the workflow is still usable.

Add footage that you created or are licensed to study and reuse. Keep study-only references outside
renderable component folders, and never ship a reference clip in client or commercial output unless
its license explicitly permits that use.
