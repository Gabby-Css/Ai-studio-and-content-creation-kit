# TRIBE v2 — optional research scoring

The kit references a research-model scoring workflow, but does not include TRIBE v2 weights, its upstream runtime, a Rust build, or an analyser web app. There is no preinstalled scoring environment or automatic download step.

If you choose to explore it, obtain and configure the relevant upstream implementation separately. Review the exact current code/model terms, including any non-commercial restrictions, before using your own or client content. Do not infer that charging no separate scoring fee makes a use permissible. See `LICENSES.md`.

Research-model predictions are experimental signals, not measured viewer attention, guaranteed retention, a virality forecast, or proof an edit improved. Validate hypotheses through appropriate real-world evaluation rather than treating model curves as audience outcomes.

The included `pipeline/generators/qa-attention-gate.cjs` can use manually identified weak timestamps for frame review and edit suggestions; it does not itself run a bundled brain-response model. Its optional language-model analysis uses your configured provider, may submit content externally, and may charge.

Review the cut yourself, mark candidate weak moments, revise, and compare. A scoring dependency is not required to read the craft guidance or inspect your own video.
