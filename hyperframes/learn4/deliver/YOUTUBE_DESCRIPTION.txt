🔴 Circle! 🟦 Square! 🔺 Triangle! Learn 8 shapes with Gizmo, Kiko and Luma in the Shape Workshop: circle, square, triangle, rectangle, oval, star, heart and diamond!
A shapes song for toddlers and preschoolers, with everyday things to spot, a "Where is it?" quiz, shape yoga and a house built from shapes.

At every stop: "What shape is this?" ... the shape pops out ... its name and one fact ("three sides!") ... three things in your home with that shape.
Then play the quiz, do shape yoga (circle arms, triangle hands, star pose, freeze!) and help build a house out of shapes!

👨‍👩‍👧 For grown-ups: sing along, point at things around you that are round, square or pointy, and pause at the quiz so your child can answer before the shape does.
What your child practices: shape names, counting sides and points, spotting shapes in everyday things, listening and answering, moving to a steady beat.

so put the 3 most important first (as above) and keep the total small (3 to 5).  It also says a video with more than 60 hashtags has ALL of them ignored.

AUDIENCE SETTING
Choose "Yes, it's made for kids". On made-for-kids videos YouTube turns off comments, end screens and cards, so the video ends with a clean
outro instead of "watch next" elements.

ALTERED / SYNTHETIC CONTENT QUESTION
The facts: the characters are animated; the singing and spoken voices and the music are AI-generated (the lead, double, harmony and children's-choir
voices are made from the Kokoro text-to-speech voices, re-timed and tuned to the melody; the band is a SoundFont orchestra). Read the question exactly
as YouTube words it in Studio and answer it honestly. The sentence "Made with animation and AI: the characters are animated, and the voices and music
are AI-generated." in the description is a safe extra.
