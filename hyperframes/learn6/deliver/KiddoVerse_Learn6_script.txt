KiddoVerse Learn #6  -  Sound It Out! Three-Letter Words
Original songs, lyrics and melodies (c) KiddoVerse. 120 BPM, C major (the key lifts as the words go on). The letter sounds are written in the script as the letters (c a t) and sung as the sounds (kuh, a, tuh).
