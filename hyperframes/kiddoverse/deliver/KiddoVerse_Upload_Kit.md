| Day | Do |
|---|---|
| 0 | Upload full video (public). Pin the comment. Share link to family/friends *who will actually watch* (watch-time counts). |
| 1 | Post Short #1 (Hook). Reply to every comment (parents can't comment on kids' videos, but Shorts shown elsewhere can get them). |

| 3 | Post Short #3 (Kiddo Wiggle). |
| 4 | Post a 6-second loop of the logo sting + "New song every week" on Community/Stories. |
| 5-7 | Check YouTube Studio: **Impressions CTR** (aim >5%) and **Average view duration**. If CTR is low -> swap thumbnail; if retention drops early -> use Short #1 as the new first 8 seconds in the next video. |

Honest expectations: nothing here can guarantee views. What helps most is **consistency** (one new song every 1-2 weeks, same characters), **a strong first 5 seconds** (the Hook short is built around one), **thumbnail + title testing**, and **many Shorts pointing to the long video**.
