TITLE (under 100 characters)
Learn 6 Colors with The Color Train | Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Purple | Songs for Toddlers

DESCRIPTION (everything between the lines)
------------------------------------------
All aboard the Color Train! The rainbow has lost its colors, so Kiko, Luma and friends ride the train to find them: red, orange, yellow,
green, blue and purple. In every stop children hear the color, see three things in that color, say it back and answer "What color is it?".
At the end the rainbow glows again and there is a "Can you find it?" color game.
Grown-ups: watch together, pause for the answers and point at things at home that are red, orange, yellow, green, blue or purple.
